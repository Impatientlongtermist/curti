Uncertainty (Monte Carlo)

Each slider above is treated as the median of a distribution instead of a fixed number. The page draws 10,000 sets of inputs, runs the chain on each, and summarises the results. Draws are on the logit scale, so values stay between 0% and 100%.

Share of each input's variation that moves together: when alignment turns out hard, misalignment, pursuit, success and timing all tend to be high and oversight low. Zero treats every input as independent.

How the total is built
fast = P(super in months) × misaligned × (1 − oversight)
       × pursues × succeeds × by 2031
slow = (1 − P(super in months)) × misaligned_slow × (1 − oversight_slow)
       × pursues_slow × succeeds_slow × by 2031_slow

given RSI     = fast + slow
unconditional = P(RSI in 2027) × given RSI
strict        = given RSI × strict share

This still leaves out misuse and race-driven conflict, so it is a misalignment-only figure. The two branches are exclusive, so their risks add without overlap.

The Monte Carlo section draws each input as a logit-normal centred on its slider, with a shared factor that moves the inputs together. The spread and the shared factor are my own judgement calls, not estimated from data, so the intervals show how the model's assumptions combine, not a calibrated forecast.
